# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Update the Regulatory Bodies page with complete contact information, addresses, and organized sections for national bodies, state councils, and consumer protection forums.

**Planned changes:**
- Add complete contact information (addresses, emails, phones) for Pharmacy Council of India, CDSCO, and Ministry of Health and Family Welfare in backend
- Add state pharmacy council entries for Haryana and Delhi with full contact details
- Add consumer protection forum entries for NCDRC, Department of Consumer Affairs, and CCPA
- Organize regulatory bodies page into three sections: National Regulatory Bodies, State Pharmacy Councils, and Consumer Protection Forums with descriptive headers
- Update regulatory body cards to display address field with location pin icon
- Add translation keys for new section headers, descriptions, and address field

**User-visible outcome:** Users can view regulatory bodies organized by type with complete contact information including addresses, making it easier to reach the appropriate authority for their needs.
